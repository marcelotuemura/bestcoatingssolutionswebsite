# Phase 2 — Brand Voice Guide

**Status:** Draft for owner approval (Phase 2)  
**Applies to:** All future copy (homepage, about, services, projects, CTAs, metadata, forms, footer)  
**Rule:** Write this guide *before* rewriting pages. If a paragraph fails the craftsman test, rewrite it.

---

## Purpose

Best Coatings Solutions should sound like an experienced craftsman speaking to a boat owner — not like a marketing department speaking to a lead funnel.

Every sentence exists to increase trust before the first phone call.

---

## Voice (who we sound like)

| Attribute | Meaning in practice |
|-----------|---------------------|
| **Honest** | Say what you know. Admit limits. Never invent proof. |
| **Calm** | No urgency tricks. No exclamation marks as persuasion. |
| **Professional** | Clear, respectful, competent — never stiff or corporate. |
| **Respectful** | Respect the vessel, the owner’s time, and competing shops. |
| **Craftsman-like** | Speak from the work: prep, patience, matching, finish. |

**Personality:** Premium · Professional · Honest · Calm · Confident · Precise · Craftsmanship-driven · Timeless  

**Never:** Corporate · Flashy · Overly aggressive · Sales-oriented · Cheap

---

## The craftsman test

After every paragraph, ask:

> Does this sound like a real craftsman speaking?

If **no**, rewrite.

Additional checks:

1. Would Marcelo say this out loud to an owner at the dock?
2. Does it explain care and process — or only claim quality?
3. Does it pressure the reader to convert, or invite a conversation?

---

## Tone by surface

| Surface | Tone |
|---------|------|
| Homepage hero | Quiet confidence. One idea. Human. |
| About / Meet Marcelo | Warm story. First person where natural. Not a résumé. |
| Philosophy | Principle-led. Emotional center of the brand. |
| Services | Outcome for the owner. Light on jargon. |
| Projects / case studies | What happened → how it was repaired → result. Specific. |
| Contact / estimate | Courteous, clear next step. No over-promise. |
| Aviation (while Coming Soon) | Honest preview. Do not compete with marine trust. |
| Metadata / SEO | Natural language. No keyword stuffing. |

---

## Writing mechanics

- Prefer **short sentences**.
- Prefer **concrete nouns** (gelcoat, hull, color match, masking) over abstractions (“solutions,” “excellence”).
- One idea per paragraph.
- Comfortable spacing; never large walls of text on the live site.
- Use “I” / “we” carefully:
  - **I** — Marcelo’s personal craft and attention.
  - **We / Best Coatings Solutions** — the company delivering the work.
- Company remains the hero; Marcelo is the craftsman behind it.
- **First name only:** Marcelo. Never use the last name in public copy.

## Voice examples

### Preferred

> We repair boats because that’s what we’ve dedicated our careers to.

> Quality is built before the paint is applied.

> I inspect the area, prepare the surface carefully, and match the finish so the repair belongs to the boat — not to a brochure.

> Free estimates are available in the Fort Lauderdale area. Other locations may need review or travel arrangements.

### Avoid

> We provide industry-leading marine coating solutions.

> State-of-the-art processes deliver unmatched customer satisfaction.

> Your trusted partner for all premium refinishing needs.

> High-quality workmanship you can count on.

---

## Banned / high-risk phrases

Do **not** use (or near-equivalents):

- Industry-leading  
- State-of-the-art  
- Customer satisfaction is our priority  
- We strive for excellence  
- Trusted partner  
- One-stop solution  
- High-quality workmanship  
- Unmatched / unbeatable / #1  
- Cutting-edge  
- World-class (unless quoting a third party with permission — do not invent)

**Use sparingly or rewrite when they appear as filler:**

- Premium (prefer showing premium through work and restraint)  
- Excellence  
- Solutions  
- Professional finish (as a hollow chip label)

Never invent:

- Certifications  
- Awards  
- Partnerships / affiliations / endorsements

- Dates or founding myths without approval  
- Employer authorization (MarineMax, Nautical Ventures, HCB, Bombardier, military work, Aisin Sin Ei are **background only** when approved for copy — never logos, never “partnered with”)

Verified facts reserved for later approved copy (Phase 3–4):

- Refinishing work involving Bombardier business jets and military helicopters (background only)
