## 1. Strategic problem

The site is technically strong and ethically careful, but emotionally incomplete.

Visitors meet a **modern template** before they meet a **craftsman**.

Phase 2 repositions Best Coatings Solutions so every later page answers:

> Why should I trust this company with my boat?

Services come **after** trust is earned.

---

## 2. Brand positioning

### What we are

A premium **marine refinishing** company — mobile, precise, honest — led by a craftsman with more than 25 years across automotive, marine, and aviation finishing.

### What we are not

- Another generic marine repair contractor website  
- A flashy sales funnel  
- An aviation-first brand (aviation remains a honest future preview)  
- A résumé site for Marcelo  

### Positioning statement (internal)

> Best Coatings Solutions restores and protects the appearance of vessels through disciplined preparation, careful color work, and finish quality you can see — with the patience of someone who has spent a career in refinishing.

### Brand promise (customer-facing, for later copy)

> Care for the boat the way a craftsman would care for his own work.

### Differentiation

| Competitor pattern | BCS stance |
|--------------------|------------|
| List services first | Tell who we are and how we work first |
| Claim “high quality” | Show process, prep, and real results |
| Busy sales CTAs | Quiet confidence: estimate + view work |
| Stock hero imagery | Real boats, real repairs, real detail |
| Founder résumé | Meet Marcelo — a story, not a CV |

Inspiration for **quality of restraint only** (do not imitate): Apple, Porsche, Rolls-Royce, Riva, Feadship, Aston Martin, Leica.

## 3. Trust before services (information architecture)

### Principle

**Identity → Trust → Craft → Proof → Offer → Action**

### Recommended homepage narrative order (for Phase 3+ implementation)

| Order | Section | Job | Notes |
|------:|---------|-----|-------|
| 1 | **Hero** | Feel luxury marine craftsmanship | Full-bleed real photo when available; temporary logo not the visual hero |
| 2 | **Who we are / Meet Marcelo (teaser)** | Human trust | Link to full About |
| 3 | **Philosophy** | Emotional center | “Quality Is Built Before the Paint Is Applied” |
| 4 | **Craftsmanship** | How we work | “Built on Experience. Driven by Detail.” — not “Why Choose Us” |
| 5 | **Selected work** | Proof | Case-study stories, not a thumbnail dump |
| 6 | **Services** | Offer | Outcome language; after trust |
| 7 | **Service area / practical fit** | Fit | South Florida; estimate policy honesty |
| 8 | **Request an Estimate** | Action | Primary conversion |

**De-emphasize on homepage until ready:** Aviation Coming Soon (footer / dedicated page only if needed), icon-chip “Why BCS” grids, Schedule Visit as hero secondary.

| Priority | Page | Role |
|----------|------|------|
| Core | Home | Trust + craft + proof + estimate |

| Core | Services | Outcomes, not feature laundry lists |
| Core | Projects | Case studies / stories |
| Core | Contact | Conversation |
| Secondary | Aviation | Honest preview while inactive |
| Supporting | Workmanship / philosophy deep pages | Extend emotional center if split |

## 4. Hero strategy

### Desired feeling

Luxury marine craftsmanship — not a technology company.

### Content brief (implementation in Phase 3)

| Element | Direction |
|---------|-----------|
| Headline | **Craftsmanship That Shows in Every Finish** |
| Support | First-person / craftsman voice: 25+ years; automotive, aviation, marine; attention to detail for boats entrusted to BCS |
| Primary CTA | **Request an Estimate** |
| Secondary CTA | **View Our Work** |
| Visual | Full-width, high-quality **real** boat or real repair photograph |

### Visual rules until final photography lands

1. Prefer the **best authentic image** available from the owner media library / vault.  
2. **No silhouettes** and no generic illustrations as the hero plane.  
3. Temporary logo may appear, but **do not build identity around it**.  
4. If no publishable photo is approved yet, use a restrained photographic holding state — never market SVG silhouettes as craft.

## 8. Services strategy

Premium companies explain **what the customer receives**.

| Instead of (label-only) | Move toward (outcome) |
|-------------------------|------------------------|
| Gelcoat Repair | Restore damaged gelcoat while preserving the appearance and integrity of your boat’s original finish. |
| Fiberglass Repair | Repair structural and cosmetic fiberglass damage with careful preparation so the area can be refinished properly. |
| Paint & Refinishing | Correct and refinish painted surfaces with controlled process and honest scope. |

**Phase 6** will rewrite every service page to this pattern. Phase 2 locks the pattern only.

## 9. Projects strategy — stories, not a gallery

Every published project should answer:

1. What happened?  
2. How was it repaired?  
3. What techniques / materials were used?  
4. What was the final result?  
5. Visuals: before / during / after when available  

Do **not** build a decorative gallery of unlabeled thumbnails.  
Do **not** invent case studies. Empty + honest remains better than fake proof until real stories are approved.

## 11. Color strategy

**Keep the dark palette.** Soften the “tech electric” impression over time.

| Role | Direction |
|------|-----------|
| Atmosphere | Deep navy / near-black |
| Surfaces | Graphite / navy raised |
| Text | Soft white / warm gray |
| Metal accent | Brushed aluminum (silver) |
| Accent | **Very restrained** — avoid bright electric blue overload |

**Avoid:** bright blue as identity, purple gradients, cream+terracotta brochure clichés, neon.

Token changes belong to Phase 10 (UI Design), guided by this strategy — not this phase.
