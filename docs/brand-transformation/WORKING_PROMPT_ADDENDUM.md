## Phase 2 owner directives (locked into strategy)

1. Sell **trust before services**.  
2. Hero → luxury marine craftsmanship; real photo; no silhouettes.  
3. Quiet craftsman voice — not marketing solutions language.  
4. About title: **Meet Marcelo**.  
5. Philosophy center: **Quality Is Built Before the Paint Is Applied**.  
6. Craft section: **Built on Experience. Driven by Detail.** (not “Why Choose Us”).  
7. Services = customer outcomes.  
8. Projects = stories, not galleries.  
9. Nav target: Home · About · Services · Projects · Aviation · Contact.  
10. Keep dark palette; restrain bright blue; prefer navy / graphite / soft white / warm gray / aluminum.  
11. Large headings, airy spacing, short paragraphs.  
12. Photography priority: real work only whenever possible.  
13. Temporary logo is temporary — do not build identity around it.  
14. CTAs: **Request an Estimate** + **View Our Work**.  
15. Feeling: “They care about doing the job correctly.”
